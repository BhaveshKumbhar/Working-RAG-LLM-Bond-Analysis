Master List of Covenants

POSITIVE (AFFIRMATIVE) COVENANTS
A. Regulatory & Corporate Compliance
1. Comply with all applicable laws, RBI regulations, SEBI regulations, Companies Act, 2013 and other regulatory requirements.
2. Maintain NBFC registration, licenses, approvals and authorizations required for business operations.
3. Comply with Fair Practices Code and Corporate Governance requirements prescribed by RBI.
4. Ensure issuance, allotment and listing of NCDs in compliance with applicable regulations.
5. Maintain all statutory filings and regulatory compliances during the tenor of the NCDs.
6. Obtain and maintain all approvals, consents, NOCs and authorizations necessary for operations and financing arrangements.

B. End Use & Utilisation Covenants
7. Utilize NCD proceeds strictly for approved purposes.
8. Restrict usage of proceeds to permitted business activities.
9. Submit CA/Statutory Auditor certified End Use Certificate within stipulated timelines.
10. Ensure no diversion of funds.
11. Ensure proceeds are not used for:
* Capital market investments.
* Equity investments.
* Real estate investments.
* Dividend payments.
* Speculative activities.
* Restricted sectors.
12. Obtain prior investor approval before any change in end-use.

C. Security Creation & Maintenance Covenants
13. Create security before or within stipulated timelines post allotment.
14. Perfect security by filing CHG-9 with ROC.
15. Register charges with ROC/CERSAI/other authorities.
16. Maintain minimum security cover at all times.
17. Replenish security cover immediately upon shortfall.
18. Provide periodic Security Cover Certificates certified by CA.
19. Replace ineligible or delinquent receivables forming part of security pool.
20. Top-up collateral if security cover falls below required levels.
21. Maintain first-ranking, exclusive and continuing charge in favour of Debenture Trustee.
22. Maintain validity and enforceability of all security documents.

D. Financial Covenants
Portfolio / Asset Quality
23. Maintain minimum CRAR/Capital Adequacy Ratio.
24. Maintain Gross NPA below prescribed thresholds.
25. Maintain Net NPA below prescribed thresholds.
26. Maintain positive profitability (PAT not negative).
27. Maintain Debt/Equity ratios within agreed limits.
28. Maintain DSCR above stipulated threshold.
29. Raise minimum equity capital within agreed timelines (where applicable).
30. Ensure no material deterioration in portfolio quality.
 
E. Reporting & Information Covenants
31. Submit annual audited financial statements.
32. Submit quarterly financial statements.
33. Submit monthly MIS reports.
34. Submit monthly bank statements.
35. Submit monthly GST returns and GST payment status.
36. Submit monthly collection reports.
37. Submit asset cover certificates.
38. Submit security cover certificates.
39. Provide periodic portfolio performance reports.
40. Provide receivables statements.
41. Furnish compliance certificates from auditors.
42. Provide information requested by Debenture Trustee or Investors.
43. Report any covenant breach immediately.

F. Event-Based Reporting Covenants
44. Notify any Event of Default or Potential Event of Default immediately.
45. Notify material litigation, arbitration or investigations.
46. Notify any regulatory action.
47. Notify winding-up, insolvency or NCLT proceedings.
48. Notify material adverse effect.
49. Notify changes in:
* Shareholding.
* Board composition.
* Senior management.
* Accounting policies.
* Constitutional documents.
50. Notify mergers, acquisitions, restructuring proposals and major transactions.

G. Governance Covenants
51. Maintain adequate internal controls.
52. Maintain anti-fraud systems.
53. Maintain AML/KYC compliance systems.
54. Maintain independent director(s) on the Board.
55. Maintain board and management control with promoters where agreed.
56. Comply with investor monitoring requirements.

H. Inspection & Monitoring Covenants
57. Permit inspection of books and records.
58. Permit investor audits and monitoring visits.
59. Cooperate with due diligence exercises.
60. Provide access to management, operations and records upon notice.

I. Shareholding & Management Covenants
61. Promoters to retain management control.
62. Promoters to continue in executive capacity.
63. Maintain minimum promoter shareholding.
64. Provide information relating to any proposed secondary sale.
65. Use promoter liquidity events for prepayment where agreed.

MASTER LIST - NEGATIVE COVENANTS
A. Capital Structure Restrictions
1. No buyback of shares.
2. No redemption of share capital.
3. No reduction of capital.
4. No defeasance or return of capital.
5. No dilution of promoter shareholding below agreed threshold.
6. No change in capital structure without lender consent.
7. No issuance of instruments adversely affecting investor rights.

B. Dividend Restrictions
8. No dividend declaration during default.
9. No dividend declaration unless adequate provisions are made.
10. No dividend declaration if debt servicing obligations remain unpaid.

C. Debt Restrictions
11. No incremental indebtedness without consent.
12. No pari passu or senior debt creation without approval.
13. No unsecured borrowing beyond agreed limits.
14. No off-balance sheet liabilities without consent.
15. No guarantees or comfort letters except in ordinary course.

D. Security & Asset Disposal Restrictions
16. No sale of secured assets.
17. No transfer of charged assets.
18. No assignment of hypothecated receivables.
19. No creation of encumbrances over secured assets.
20. No disposal of material assets/business/divisions.
21. No compromise with creditors affecting security.

E. Merger / M&A Restrictions
22. No merger.
23. No demerger.
24. No amalgamation.
25. No consolidation.
26. No restructuring.
27. No scheme of arrangement.
28. No corporate reorganization.
29. No acquisition or joint venture without consent.
30. No business transfer impacting investor interests.

F. Business Restrictions
31. No change in business line.
32. No diversification outside core business.
33. No material change in business model.
34. No new major business activity without consent.
35. No cessation of business.

G. Constitutional Document Restrictions
36. No amendment to MOA/AOA without consent.
37. Exception only for increase in authorized share capital or agreed investor-driven amendments.

H. Related Party Restrictions
38. No related party transactions except:
* At arm's length.
* In ordinary course of business.
39. No promoter loans or advances prejudicial to debenture holders.
40. No value leakage to promoters.

I. Shareholding & Control Restrictions
41. No change in control.
42. No change in management control.
43. No dilution of promoter stake below agreed level.
44. No transfer of promoter shares without consent.
45. No change in voting rights structure.
46. No change in board control.


J. Transactions Outside Ordinary Course
47. No loans outside ordinary course of business.
48. No investments outside ordinary course.
49. No guarantees outside ordinary course.
50. No unusual transactions affecting credit profile.

K. Insolvency / Restructuring Restrictions
51. No voluntary winding-up.
52. No creditor compromise arrangements.
53. No insolvency filings.
54. No debt restructuring without lender approval.
55. No actions prejudicial to enforceability of security.
